How & Why to Build Healthy Soil

Date: Friday, September 25 — 3:00–4:30 PM (Eastern Time)

Healthy soil is the foundation of healthy ecosystems, healthy water cycles, and a stable climate. In this webinar, environmental reporter Hart Hagan explores how soil functions as a living system and why rebuilding soil health is one of the most practical and effective ways to restore landscapes.

Participants will learn how soil biology, organic matter, water infiltration, and land stewardship practices work together to improve ecosystem resilience. The webinar will also examine how healthy soil helps retain water, reduce erosion, support biodiversity, and strengthen local food systems.
